Morning Mobility
DAILY · ~20 MIN · EVERY MORNING
1
Cat-Cow
90 seconds
Start on hands and knees. On inhale, drop belly, lift chest and tailbone (cow). On exhale, round spine toward ceiling, tuck chin and pelvis (cat). Move one vertebra at a time from tailbone up through your neck. Most people rush this — don't. Slow is the whole point.
2
World's Greatest Stretch
90 seconds each side
Step into a deep lunge, front foot flat. Place same-side hand inside foot on the ground. Drive opposite arm toward the ceiling, rotating your thoracic spine — eyes follow the hand. Return hand to floor. Option: straighten the front leg for a hamstring stretch. Return to lunge. Flow through 5–6 reps each side. You should feel hips, hip flexors, thoracic spine, and hamstrings — all in one movement.
3
Half-Kneeling Hip Rotator Stretch
90 seconds each side
Sit on floor, left leg flat and relaxed. Cross right leg over, right foot flat, knee bent 90°. Sit tall first — do not let your lower back round. Place right elbow on inside of right knee. Inhale to lengthen spine, exhale and slowly rotate torso left, using elbow to gently push knee for leverage. Hold end range for 3–5 slow breaths. You'll feel piriformis and deep hip rotators. Switch sides.
4
Glute Bridge with Band
2 minutes
Band just above knees. Lie on back, feet flat, knees bent. Push knees slightly out against band — feel the glute meds engage. Drive hips toward ceiling, squeeze glutes hard at the top. Hold 2 seconds. Lower with control. This is activation — focus on feeling the glutes fire, not how heavy the band is.
5
Banded Ankle Circles + Calf Stretch
90 seconds each side
Lift foot slightly. Draw the largest possible circle with your toes — ankle joint only, not the whole leg. 5 circles each direction. Then step one foot back, heel flat on ground, back leg straight — upper calf stretch (gastroc). Then bend the back knee slightly, heel still pressing down — stretch drops into Achilles and soleus. Hold each position 20–30 seconds.
6
Morning Meditation
5 minutes · Peloton
Immediately after the physical sequence. Sit comfortably, close your eyes. Let the body settle before the day begins. Open Peloton and select a 5-minute morning meditation.

Eccentric Heel Drops
DAILY · 5 MIN · ACHILLES RESILIENCE
Why This MattersYour Achilles was screaming at the end of your marathon. This 5-minute daily exercise is your primary Achilles and calf resilience tool for the 50K. Non-negotiable — even on rest days.
1
Setup
Starting position
Stand on the edge of a step or curb with the balls of your feet. Both heels hanging off the edge. Hold a wall or railing for balance if needed.
2
Eccentric Lower
3 × 15 reps each leg
Rise up on BOTH feet. Shift weight to ONE foot. Slowly lower your heel below the step over 3–4 seconds — this slow lowering is the entire exercise. Use both feet to rise back up, single foot to lower. That is one rep. Never rush the descent.
3
Modification if Sore
First week option
Do them flat-footed — same movement pattern but without dropping the heel below parallel. Reduces eccentric load while still providing stimulus. Use this if soreness from your first sessions is significant.
